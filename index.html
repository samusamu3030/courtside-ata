Welcome to the war room

Twelve managers. One trophy. Zero mercy. This is the film room for All Things Athletic: your odds this week and next, who to start, who's hurt, who's sitting on waivers, and which trades will fleece your friends. Pick your squad and get to work.

Every number comes off the ESPN tape and thousands of simulated weeks, with the latest scoop from Locked On Fantasy Basketball. Claude refreshes it every few days. Use it, ignore it, blame it when you lose. But if you start a guy who's out with your bench full of healthy players, that one's on you.

    How this works
    • Each team's build is Claude's read of its roster: categories where it ranks 10th or worse are treated as punts. The real managers' plans may differ.
    • Win chances come from simulating each week thousands of times with this season's stats.
    • Pickups must play 21+ minutes this season; free agents are preferred over waiver claims. Each team gets 4 adds a week.
    • Locks are at each day's first tip-off, usually about 8 AM Malaysia time.
    • Sources: ESPN league data, Locked On Fantasy Basketball. Prepared by Claude; numbers are a snapshot from the time shown at the top.
