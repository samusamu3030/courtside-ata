What this site is

A game-day helper for every team in our fantasy basketball league. Tap a team below to see its odds for this week and next, a day-by-day lineup, injuries, the best free agents and trade ideas with other teams.

Everything is worked out from ESPN league data and simulated weeks, with news from Locked On Fantasy Basketball. Claude prepares it, and it's refreshed every few days. It's a guide, not a rulebook: each manager makes their own calls.

How this works
  • Each team's build is Claude's read of its roster: categories where it ranks 10th or worse are treated as punts. The real managers' plans may differ.
  • Win chances come from simulating each week thousands of times with this season's stats.
  • Pickups must play 21+ minutes this season; free agents are preferred over waiver claims. Each team gets 4 adds a week.
  • Locks are at each day's first tip-off, usually about 8 AM Malaysia time.
  • Sources: ESPN league data, Locked On Fantasy Basketball. Prepared by Claude; numbers are a snapshot from the time shown at the top.
